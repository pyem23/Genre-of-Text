Understanding 💡

What a procedure text is, why we need it, and where we meet it.

Let's unlock the secret of every "how to"!
📖 Definition

What is a procedure text?

“A procedure text is a text that tells the reader how to make, use, or do something through a series of steps in the correct order.

IDProcedure text adalah teks yang memberi petunjuk cara membuat, menggunakan, atau melakukan sesuatu melalui langkah-langkah yang berurutan.

🎯 Social function

Why do people write it?

🧭

To instruct

Tell the reader how to make or do something, step by step.

⚙️

To explain use

Show how to use or operate a tool, a device, or an app.

🛡️

To guide safely

Help the reader get the right result without mistakes or danger.

Four kinds of mission

⚔️ Don't mix them up!

Procedure vs Recount

PROCEDURE

Purpose
Tells how to make, use or do something
Structure
Goal → Materials → Steps → (Closing)
Tense
Simple present · imperative (Cut, Add, Press)
Example
🍳 How to Make Nasi Goreng
VS

RECOUNT

Purpose
Retells past events or experiences
Structure
Orientation → Events → Reorientation
Tense
Simple past (went, cooked, pressed)
Example
🏔️ My Trip to Mount Lawu

🍳 How to Make Simple Nasi Goreng

GoalNasi goreng is a favourite breakfast in many Indonesian homes. Here is an easy way to cook a plate for yourself in about 15 minutes.
Materials
Ingredients
  • 1 plate of cold cooked rice
  • 1 egg
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 3 shallots
  • 1 tablespoon of sweet soy sauce
  • a pinch of salt
  • 2 tablespoons of cooking oil
Tools
  • a frying pan
  • a spatula
  • a knife
  • a plate
Steps
  1. First, peel and slice the garlic and shallots thinly.
  2. Then, heat the oil in a frying pan over medium heat.
  3. Next, fry the garlic and shallots for about one minute until they smell good.
  4. After that, crack the egg into the pan and scramble it quickly.
  5. Add the rice and stir well so that it does not stick to the pan.
  6. Pour in the sweet soy sauce, sprinkle some salt, and mix everything evenly.
  7. Finally, cook for two more minutes, then turn off the stove.
ClosingServe your nasi goreng while it is still hot. Enjoy your breakfast! Be careful: don't leave the stove on when you are not cooking.

📦 Segmentation

What goes inside each part?

① Goal / Aimthe mission
A title: How to + verb · (optional) 1–2 intro sentences: what you will make or do, why it is useful, how long it takes
Title + 0–2 sentences"How to Make…", "How to Use…"
② Materialsthe gear
Ingredients, tools, equipment or requirements, with exact quantities and sizes
A list, not a storyUse numbers: 2 cloves, 250 ml
③ Stepsthe method
Actions in the correct order · one main action per step · details of how long, how much, how, where · warnings
3–10 numbered stepsStart with a verb or sequencer
④ Closingoptional
The result, how to serve or check it, a final tip, or a friendly wish
1–2 short sentences"Your … is ready. Enjoy!"

Language Features 🔤

The power-ups that make instructions clear and easy to follow.

Collect all six power-ups! ⚡
⚡ Power-ups

Six language features

📣

Imperative sentences

Commands that start with a base verb. The subject you is hidden.

Cut the onion. · Press the button.
🔢

Sequencers

Show the order of the steps.

first, then, next, after that, finally
🎛️

Adverbials

Tell how, how long, and where to do each step.

slowly, for 3 minutes, in a pan
⚖️

Quantities & precise nouns

Exact amounts and names of materials.

2 cloves of garlic, 250 ml of water
🔧

Action verbs

Specific verbs for each kind of task.

chop, stir, fold, tap, plug in
⚠️

Simple present & warnings

General truths, don't, make sure, should.

Don't touch it. · Make sure it is off.
