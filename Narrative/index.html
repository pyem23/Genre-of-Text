02 CHAPTER 02

Understanding 💡

What a narrative is, why we tell it, and its types.

Legend, fairy tale or fantasy? Let's find out! ✨
📖 Definition

What is a narrative text?

“A narrative text is an imaginative story that tells a series of events with a problem (conflict) and its solution, to entertain the reader and often to teach a moral value.

IDTeks naratif adalah cerita imajinatif berisi rangkaian peristiwa dengan konflik dan penyelesaiannya, untuk menghibur pembaca dan sering menyampaikan pesan moral.

🎯 Social function

Why do people tell stories?

🎭

To entertain

Amuse the reader with exciting events, surprises and emotions.

🌱

To teach a lesson

Share a moral value: honesty, kindness, courage, respect…

🏮

To keep culture alive

Pass on folklore, beliefs and imagination to the next generation.

Narrative vs Recount

📖 NARRATIVE

Purpose
To entertain and teach a moral value
Story
Imaginative: legend, fairy tale, fantasy
Structure
Orientation → Complication → Resolution → (Coda)
Key sign
There is a conflict that must be solved
VS

🗓️ RECOUNT

Purpose
To retell past events and inform
Story
Real experience: holiday, trip, event
Structure
Orientation → Events → Reorientation
Key sign
A series of events, no main conflict needed

🔑 Quick test: Is there a problem that must be solved? Yes → narrative · No, just real events in order → recount

📦 Segmentation

What goes inside each part?

① Orientationthe beginning
Introduces the characters (who), the setting (where & when) and the situation
1 paragraph“Once upon a time, there lived…”
② Complicationthe problem
A problem or conflict starts; tension rises until the climax, the most exciting moment
1–3 paragraphs“One day… Suddenly… However…”
③ Resolutionthe ending
The problem is solved, for better or worse: the ending can be happy or sad
1 paragraph“Finally… In the end…”
④ Codaoptional
The moral value, or what happens after the story / how the characters change
1–2 sentences“The moral of the story is that…”
04 CHAPTER 04

Language Features 🪄

The magic words that bring a story to life.

Past tense is my magic spell! 🪄
⚡ Magic words

Six language features

⏪

Simple past tense

The story happened in the past: V2 (regular -ed & irregular).

lived · turned · ran · became
🔄

Past continuous tense

An action in progress, often interrupted by another event.

While he was sleeping, the giant came.
🔗

Time connectives

Order the events and signal a change.

Once upon a time · One day · Suddenly · Finally
🏃

Action & saying verbs

Show what characters do and how they speak.

threw · chased · shouted · whispered
💬

Direct & indirect speech

Bring the characters' voices into the story.

“Help me!” she cried. / She cried for help.
🎨

Descriptive words

Adjectives and adverbs that paint the characters and scenes.

a greedy giant · ran quickly

OrientationMalin Kundang · Once upon a time, in a small fishing village in West Sumatra, there lived a poor widow and her only son, Malin Kundang. Malin was a clever and hard-working boy.
ComplicationOne day, Malin sailed away on a merchant ship. Years later, he became a rich merchant. When his ship landed at the village, his mother ran to the harbour and hugged him happily. However, Malin was ashamed of her. “I don’t know this old woman!” he shouted, and he pushed her away.
ResolutionHeartbroken, the mother prayed, “If he is really my son, turn him into stone!” Suddenly, a terrible storm hit the ship. Finally, Malin knelt on the beach and slowly turned into stone.
CodaUntil today, people can see a stone shaped like a kneeling man at Air Manis Beach. The moral of the story is that we must never disrespect our parents.
