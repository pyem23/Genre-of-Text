02 LEVEL 02

Understanding 💡

What an exposition is, why we write it, and its two types.

Opinions are cheap. Arguments win! 💪
📖 Definition

What is an exposition text?

“An exposition text is a text that presents the writer's opinion on an issue and supports it with arguments in order to persuade the reader.

IDTeks eksposisi adalah teks yang menyampaikan pendapat penulis tentang suatu isu, didukung dengan argumen, untuk meyakinkan pembaca.

🎯 Social function

Why do people write it?

🎯

To take a position

State a clear opinion (the thesis) about an issue.

🧱

To argue

Support the opinion with reasons, facts and examples.

📣

To persuade

Make the reader agree, or even take action.

⚔️ Two types

Hortatory vs Analytical

🔥 HORTATORY

Purpose
Persuades the reader that something should or should not be done
Structure
Thesis → Arguments → Recommendation
Ending
Tells the reader what to do (should, must, let's)
Example
🥤 Bring Your Own Tumbler!
VS

🔍 ANALYTICAL

Purpose
Persuades the reader that an idea is the case (true or important)
Structure
Thesis → Arguments → Reiteration
Ending
Restates the thesis in new words, with no command
Example
📚 Why Reading Matters for Teenagers

🔑 Quick test: does the ending ask the reader to DO something? Yes → hortatory · No, it just restates → analytical

📦 Segmentation

What goes inside each part?

① Thesisthe position
Short background of the issue + the writer's position, stated clearly
1 paragraph"I strongly believe that…"
② Argumentsthe proof
2–4 points, each with elaboration: a reason, fact, example or explanation
1 paragraph per point"Firstly… Moreover…"
③ Recommendation🔥 hortatory
What should be done and by whom: a clear call to action
1 paragraph"Therefore, … should …" · "Let's …!"
③ Reiteration🔍 analytical
Restates the thesis in new words and sums up the points, no command
1 paragraph"In conclusion, it is clear that…"
04 LEVEL 04

Language Features 🔤

The words that turn an opinion into a convincing argument.

Choose your words like weapons! ⚡
⚡ Power-ups

Six language features

🕒

Simple present tense

For facts, general truths and present opinions.

Plastic harms sea animals.
🧠

Thinking verbs & opinion phrases

Show that this is the writer's view.

I believe · I think · In my opinion
⚖️

Modals & calls to action

should, must, need to, let's ask for action; can, may show possibility.

We should… · Let's…!
🔗

Connectives

Order and link the arguments and the conclusion.

Firstly · Moreover · Therefore
🔥

Evaluative words

Adjectives that judge how good or bad something is.

important · harmful · beneficial
📦

Abstract & general nouns

Ideas and whole groups, not one specific thing.

pollution · health · students
🖍️ Highlighter👆 nyalakan warna, bisa lebih dari satu

Spot the features

ThesisBring Your Own Tumbler! Every day, many students buy drinks in plastic cups and bottles at school. I strongly believe that every student should bring their own tumbler. It is a small habit with a big impact.
Arguments

Firstly, bringing a tumbler reduces plastic waste. Most plastic cups are used for only a few minutes, but they can stay in the environment for many years.

Secondly, it saves money. Students do not need to buy bottled water every day, so they can spend their pocket money on other things.

Moreover, an insulated tumbler keeps drinks cold or warm for hours. It is a practical choice for long school days.

RecommendationTherefore, students should start bringing their own tumblers, and the school should provide free drinking-water refill stations. Let's make our school plastic-free, starting tomorrow!
