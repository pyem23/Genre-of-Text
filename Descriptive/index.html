02 CASE 02

Understanding 💡

What a descriptive text is, why we write it, and what we describe.

Words can work like a camera! 📸
📖 Definition

What is a descriptive text?

“A descriptive text is a text that describes a specific place, object, person or animal in detail, so that the reader can imagine what it looks, sounds, feels or is like.

IDTeks deskriptif adalah teks yang menggambarkan tempat, benda, orang, atau hewan tertentu secara rinci, sehingga pembaca dapat membayangkan wujud dan ciri-cirinya.

🎯 Social function

Why do people describe things?

🔎

To identify

Show the special features of one particular thing, place or person.

🖼️

To paint a picture

Help the reader see, hear and feel it in their mind.

📣

To introduce or promote

Present a place, a product or a person in brochures, ads and profiles.

Descriptive vs Report

📸 DESCRIPTIVE

Topic
One specific thing: my cat Mochi, our library, Borobudur
Purpose
To describe its special features
Structure
Identification → Description
Language
Personal & vivid: my favourite, as soft as…
VS

📊 REPORT

Topic
A general group: cats, libraries, temples
Purpose
To present general facts scientifically
Structure
General classification → Description
Language
Objective & technical: mammals, species

🔑 Quick test: Is it about ONE particular thing? → descriptive · About a whole group or kind? → report

📦 Segmentation

What goes inside each part?

① Identificationthe wide shot
Names what / who it is, where it is, and why it is special to the writer
1 short paragraph“… is my favourite …” · “Let me introduce …”
② Description🏫 place
Location details, parts & layout, atmosphere (five senses), activities there
1 aspect per paragraph“There are … in the middle …”
② Description🎒 object
Appearance (size, shape, colour, material), parts, function & special quality
1 aspect per paragraph“It is made of …” · “It has …”
② Description🧑 person
Physical appearance, personality (with an example), habits / hobbies
1 aspect per paragraph“She has …” · “He is always …”
04 CASE 04

Language Features 🎨

The words that turn a plain sentence into a clear picture.

Adjectives are my colour filters! 🌈
⚡ Detective tools

Six language features

🕒

Simple present tense

Facts that are true now and routines.

The library has six shelves.
🎯

Specific participant

One particular thing or person, not a whole group.

my backpack · Dimas · our canteen
🎨

Adjectives & noun phrases

Several adjectives in the right order before a noun.

a big navy-blue canvas backpack
🧱

have/has & there is/are

Show what something owns or what is in a place.

He has curly hair. · There are four tables.
👃

Linking & sensing verbs

is, looks, feels, smells, sounds, tastes + adjective.

The room feels cool.
🪞

Similes & comparisons

Compare to make the picture stronger.

as quiet as a forest · taller than me
