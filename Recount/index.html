02 PART 02

Understanding 💡

What a recount is, why we write it, and its four types.

Let's find out what a recount really is!
📖 Definition

What is a recount text?

“A recount text is a text that retells past events or experiences in the order they happened: chronological order.

IDRecount text adalah teks yang menceritakan kembali peristiwa atau pengalaman di masa lalu secara berurutan sesuai waktu terjadinya.

🎯 Social function

Why do people write it?

📢

To inform

Tell the reader what happened, clearly and in order.

😂

To entertain

Share an interesting or funny experience with the reader.

💭

To reflect

Look back on an experience and share a lesson.

🌍 Real life

Where will you use it?

Four types of recount

⚔️ Don't mix them up!

Recount vs Narrative

RECOUNT

Purpose
Retells real (or realistic) past events
Structure
Orientation → Events → Reorientation
Focus
The sequence of events
Example
🏔️ My Trip to Mount Lawu
VS

NARRATIVE

Purpose
Entertains with a story that has a conflict
Structure
Orientation → Complication → Resolution
Focus
A problem and how it is solved
Example
🪨 Malin Kundang
03 PART 03

Generic Structure 🧩

Three stops on the trail: where it starts, what happens, and how it ends.

Every good trail has a start, a middle and a top!
🧭 Explore👆 klik bagian teks atau tombol

Three parts of the trail

🏔️ A Sunrise Hike to Mount Lawu

OrientationLast June, during the school holiday, my cousin Raka and I went hiking to Mount Lawu, on the border of Central Java and East Java. We wanted to see the famous sunrise from Hargo Dumilah, the highest peak.
Event 1We left Solo on Friday afternoon by motorbike and arrived at the Cemoro Sewu basecamp at around 5 p.m. After we registered and checked our equipment, we started climbing at 7 p.m.
Event 2The trail was steep and rocky, so we walked slowly. While we were walking, the wind blew harder. At midnight, we stopped at Post 3 and rested in a small shelter. It was so cold that my fingers felt stiff.
Event 3At 3 a.m., we continued the climb. Finally, we reached the peak just before the sun came up. The sky slowly turned orange, and a sea of clouds spread below us.
ReorientationWe came down the mountain later that morning, tired but happy. It was the most unforgettable holiday I have ever had, and I learned that patience is the key to reaching the top.
📦 Segmentation

What goes inside each part?

① Orientationsets the scene
Who · when · where · what · why (optional)
1 paragraph, 2–4 sentencesOpen with a time phrase: "Last June, …"
② Eventsstep by step
Actions in time order · details (time, place, how) · small reactions or feelings · a surprise moment (optional)
2–4 paragraphs, one stage eachStart with First, Then, After that, Finally
③ Reorientationclosing
How it ended · personal feeling · comment · lesson learned · hope (optional)
1 short paragraphAn ending + a feeling is enough

🧰 Toolkit

Six language features

⏪

Simple past tense

The events are finished. Regular verbs add -ed; irregular verbs change form.

arrive → arrived · go → went
🔗

Connectives

Link events in time order or show cause and result.

first, then, while, so, finally
🕒

Time adverbials

Tell when each event happened.

last June, at midnight, at 3 a.m.
📍

Place adverbials

Tell where the events happened.

at the basecamp, in a shelter
👫

Specific participants

Real, particular people, often I / we.

my cousin Raka and I, we
💖

Action & feeling verbs

Actions in the events; feelings mostly at the end.

climbed, reached · felt, learned
